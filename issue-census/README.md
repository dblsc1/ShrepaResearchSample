# Issue census behind the figures in the paper's introduction

`issue-census-2026-11-16.md` is the classification (in Chinese) of the GitHub issues of Paperclip and Bernstein that concern
the organization or the governance rules.

## Paperclip: 52 of 92 (57%)

| Class in the census | Issues | Ask to extend or customize | The rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problems met when changing roles or the organization | 17 | 9 | 8 are defects |
| Requests to attach rules or permissions differently | 45 | 43 | 2 are defects |
| Rules not enforced or bypassed | 30 | 0 | gaps in enforcement, not requests |
| Total | 92 | 52 | |

Limits of this count, as measured:

- 6 of the 52 are borderline (the title reads as a defect, the substance is "this configuration cannot be made"). Without
  them the count is 46 of 92 (50%).
- The 92 are a lower bound: of 2,025 candidate issues only about 150 were read in full.
- "Asks to extend or customize" was judged from the title and the one-line reason of the census, not by reading each issue
  again; of the third class only 10 of the 30 one-line reasons were seen.

## Bernstein: 22 of 26 filed by the maintainer

26 issues concern the organization or the governance rules; 22 of them were filed by the maintainer. 8 of the 26 are feature
requests, 18 are gaps in enforcement.
